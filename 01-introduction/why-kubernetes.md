Before Kubernetes, managing containers manually became very difficult when applications scaled.

## Problems Without Kubernetes

- Hard to manage thousands of containers
- No automatic scaling
- No automatic recovery from failures
- Complex networking between containers
- Manual deployments

## How Kubernetes Solves These Problems

|Problem|Kubernetes Solution|
|---|---|
|Container crashes|Self-healing restarts containers|
|High traffic|Auto scaling|
|Deployment updates|Rolling updates|
|Service communication|Built-in service discovery|
|Infrastructure management|Automated orchestration|

## Real World Use Cases

Companies using Kubernetes:

- Netflix
- Spotify
- Airbnb
- Google
- Shopify

These companies use Kubernetes to **run large-scale microservices applications**.
